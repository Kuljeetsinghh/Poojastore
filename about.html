About Umesh Pooja Store

Welcome to Umesh Pooja Store, your trusted destination for Pooja Samagri and religious essentials in Ranchi, Jharkhand.

We provide a wide range of quality products for daily pooja, festivals, religious ceremonies, temples, and special occasions. Our goal is to make all your devotional and pooja essentials easily available in one place.

We serve both Wholesale & Retail customers with a focus on quality products, reasonable prices and reliable service. Whether you are a household devotee, a pandit, a temple trustee or a bulk buyer – we have the right products for you.

Our products include Mala & Jap items, Ganga Jal, Kalawa & Sacred Threads, Diya & Lighting, Dhoop & Incense, Pooja Mats & Cloths, Pooja Kits, Festival Items and much more – all under one roof.
